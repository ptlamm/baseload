About me

I'm Liam, an environmental engineer turned sustainable finance graduate, based in London and originally from Vietnam.

Each week, Baseload places public news on the investment cycle to show where capital goes next, with a focus on emerging markets and ASEAN. Summaries are my reading of other people's reporting, and every story links to its source. Nothing here is investment advice.

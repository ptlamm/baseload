- grids (stage 2, Early capital): supports current stage, with a push forward. The Johns Hopkins study projects US transformer shortfalls of about 15% (30% for other grid equipment) within four years, a stage 3 symptom, but there was no grid fund close or new private transmission opening this week. Stay at 2; move to 3 once a large grid fund closes or private ownership widens. Reading slightly firmer than last week.

- storage (stage 4, Crowded): supports current stage, with a push towards mature. ### Proposed new theme
